# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussions focused on the work.
Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated.

To report unacceptable behaviour, use GitHub's private reporting or contact the maintainer through the email
address on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
